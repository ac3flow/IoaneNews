    How stories are verified and scored

    Three agents run in sequence every five minutes. The Research agent collects reporting from a registry of wires, regulators, central banks, statistical offices and specialist press. The Copy editor polishes tone and clarity without touching a single number. The Fact-checker scores the story from 0 to 100.
